TOOL-CALLING BOUNDARIES

The Model Itself Can Be Steered by Anything It Reads

A model can't reliably tell its developer's instructions apart from text it merely retrieved. The boundary that matters isn't the model's intent — it's what the surrounding system structurally permits.

Two boundaries matter here, and they're structural, not behavioral: the read gate (what untrusted content is allowed to reach the model at all — allowlisted domains, no private IP ranges, no blind redirects) and the action gate (what the model is allowed to do as a result — scoped tools, human approval before anything irreversible). Fetched content is treated as data, never as instructions, on both sides of the model.
