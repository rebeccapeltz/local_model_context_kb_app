# Homemade Dog Food Recipe

### Simple Homemade Dog Food Recipe

**Ingredients:**
- 1 lb ground cooked chicken (no seasoning, no added fats)
- � cup cooked brown rice or sweet potato
- 1/4 cup cooked lentils or brown lentils
- 1 tbsp olive oil or flaxseed oil
- 1 tsp cooked, chopped carrots
- 1/2 cup cooked pumpkin

**Instructions:**
1. Cook all ingredients thoroughly (chicken, rice, lentils) until soft.
2. Combine everything and mix well.
3. Serve warm or cooled, depending on your dog’s preference.

**Note:** Avoid onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes, and anything with artificial additives.
