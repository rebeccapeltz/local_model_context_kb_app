# Homemade Cat Food

Homemade Cat Food Recipe

Ingredients (for 1–2 servings):
- 1 cup cooked, lean ground turkey or chicken (no seasonings)
- ½ cup cooked, soft-boiled eggs (scrambled or mashed)
- ¼ cup cooked, brown rice or oatmeal (optional for fiber and texture)
- 1–2 tbsp canned pumpkin (for fiber + digestion aid — *not to be confused with sweet potato*)
- ¼ cup cooked, finely chopped carrots (optional for nutrients)

Optional Additions:
- 1–2 tbsp cooked lentils or quinoa (for protein)
- A small amount of grated zucchini for hydration

Instructions:
1. Cook base ingredients: Boil or steam the chicken/turkey, cook eggs until soft and mash slightly. Cook rice/oats separately.
2. Combine: Mix all ingredients thoroughly with a spoon or fork until blended into a soft, moist paste.
3. Moisten: Add water or broth if needed to achieve desired consistency (cats prefer moist food).
4. Cool: Let cool to room temperature before feeding.
5. Serve: Offer in a small bowl or cup — avoid overfeeding, especially with high-protein foods.

Tips:
- Feed this daily or every other day — don’t overfeed.
- Avoid onions, garlic, chocolate, caffeine, and dairy (cats can't digest lactose).
- Always consult your vet before introducing new foods — especially if your cat has health issues.

Storage:
- Store in the fridge for up to 3 days.
- Freeze portions if you want to prep ahead — thaw overnight in the fridge.

This recipe is balanced, safe for most cats (with vet approval), and packed with protein, hydration, and fiber. Enjoy your homemade cat treats!
