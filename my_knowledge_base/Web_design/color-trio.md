# Color Trio

Three versatile professional color trios for slide decks:
1. Deep Navy (#0A2463) – strong base, authoritative vibe.
2. Soft Slate Gray (#7D8C99) – subtle contrast, good for body text.
3. Warm Gold Accent (#D4AF37) – adds optimism, draws attention.

Combine: Background navy, primary text slate gray, gold accents on key elements. Ensure accessibility with 4.5:1 contrast ratio.
