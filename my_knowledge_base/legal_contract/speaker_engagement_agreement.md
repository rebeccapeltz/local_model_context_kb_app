# Speaker Engagement Agreement

**This Agreement** is made effective as of [Date], by and between:

**The Speaker:** [Your Full Name/Company Name], located at [Your Address] (“Speaker”).
**The Client:** [Organization Name], located at [Organization Address] (“Client”).

### 1. Scope of Services
The Speaker agrees to deliver one (1) online presentation/talk regarding the topic of **[Insert Topic Name]**.
*   **Duration:** The talk shall be approximately 60 minutes in length.
*   **Platform:** The talk will be conducted online via [e.g., Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, etc.].
*   **Date & Time:** [Insert Date] at [Insert Time and Time Zone].

### 2. Compensation
In consideration for the services provided, the Client agrees to pay the Speaker a flat fee of **$[Amount]**.
*   **Payment Schedule:** [e.g., 50% deposit due upon signing; 50% due on the day of the talk / within 14 days of completion].
*   **Payment Method:** [e.g., PayPal, Bank Transfer, Stripe].

### 3. Intellectual Property & Distribution Rights
*   **Ownership:** The Speaker retains all ownership and intellectual property rights to the content, materials, and concepts presented during the talk.
*   **Right to Share Notes:** The Speaker expressly reserves the right to share, distribute, and publish notes, summaries, and educational materials derived from the talk online (e.g., via a personal website, social media, or newsletter).
*   **Client Usage:** [Optional: Specify if the Client can record the talk. e.g., "The Client may record the session for internal use only and may not distribute the recording publicly without written consent from the Speaker."]

### 4. Cancellation Policy
*   **By Client:** If the Client cancels the engagement less than [e.g., 7 days] before the scheduled date, the Speaker shall retain the deposit.
*   **By Speaker:** If the Speaker is unable to perform due to an emergency, they will attempt to reschedule the talk at a mutually convenient time or provide a full refund of any deposits paid.

### 5. Independent Contractor Status
The Speaker is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create a partnership, joint venture, or employer-employee relationship.

### 6. Limitation of Liability
The Speaker shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising out of the delivery of the talk or the distribution of notes.
