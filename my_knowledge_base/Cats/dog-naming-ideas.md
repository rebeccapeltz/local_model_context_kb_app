# Dog Naming Ideas

# Dog Naming Ideas (White & Tan)

Here are some name ideas for a white dog with floppy tan ears, categorized by vibe:

### 1. Inspired by Colors (White & Tan)
* Latte
* Chai
* Mochi
* Biscuit
* Cannoli
* Tofu
* Casper
* Honey

### 2. Nature & Earthy Tones
* Willow
* Aspen
* Maple
* Daisy
* Clover
* Cedar

### 3. The "Teddy Bear" Vibe
* Teddy
* Bear
* Button
* Pip
* Waffles

### 4. Elegant & Sophisticated
* Pearl
* Finn
* Juno
* Luna
* Atlas

### Tips for choosing:
1. The "Backdoor Test": Shout the name out the back door to see if it feels natural.
2. Two-Syllable Rule: Dogs often respond best to two-syllable names.
3. Observe Personality: Wait a few days to see if they are more of a "Lazy Mochi" or a "Spunky Pip."
