# Banana Bread Ingredients

# Banana Bread Ingredients

### Core Ingredients:
* Ripe Bananas: 3 to 4 large bananas
* Butter: ½ cup (1 stick) melted butter, or vegetable oil
* Sugar: ¾ cup to 1 cup (white, brown, or a mix)
* Egg: 1 large egg, beaten
* Vanilla Extract: 1 teaspoon
* Baking Soda: 1 teaspoon
* Salt: A pinch (about ¼ teaspoon)
* All-Purpose Flour: 1½ cups

### Optional Mix-ins:
* Cinnamon: 1 teaspoon
* Walnuts or Pecans: ½ cup, chopped
* Chocolate Chips: ½ cup (semi-sweet)
* Shredded Coconut: ¼ cup

**Tip:** Use very ripe, brown bananas for the best flavor and texture!
