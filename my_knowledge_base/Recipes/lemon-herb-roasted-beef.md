# Lemon Herb Roasted Beef

# Lemon-Herb Roasted Beef with Mediterranean Vegetables

*Serves: 4 | Prep time: 15 mins | Cook time: 20–25 mins*

## Ingredients

**The Meat:**
* 1 lb (450g) Beef tenderloin or sirloin steak (cut into 1-inch cubes)
* 2 tbsp Extra virgin olive oil
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tsp dried oregano
* 1 tsp dried thyme
* Salt and cracked black pepper to taste

**The Vegetables:**
* 1 large zucchini, sliced into half-moons
* 1 red bell pepper, chopped into chunks
* 1 red onion, cut into wedges
* 1 cup cherry tomatoes
* Juice of half a lemon

**The Finish:**
* A handful of fresh parsley, chopped
* Optional: 2 tbsp crumbled feta cheese (for a Greek twist)

## Instructions

1. **Marinate the Beef:**
In a medium bowl, toss the beef cubes with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper. Let it sit for 10–15 minutes while you prep the vegetables.

2. **Prepare the Vegetables:**
In a large roasting pan or a heavy-bottomed skillet, toss the zucchini, bell pepper, red onion, and cherry tomatoes with the remaining lemon juice and a drizzle of olive oil. Season lightly with salt.

3. **Sear/Roast:**
* *Option A (Oven):* Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Spread the vegetables on a sheet pan. Add the beef cubes to the pan. Roast for 15–20 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the beef is cooked to your liking and vegetables are tender.
* *Option B (Stovetop):* Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat with a splash of oil. Brown the beef cubes quickly (about 3–4 minutes). Remove the beef and set aside. Add the vegetables to the same pan and sauté for 8–10 minutes until softened. Return the beef to the pan for 1 minute to reheat.

4. **Final Touch:**
Remove from heat. Sprinkle with fresh parsley and the crumbled feta (if using). Serve immediately.

## Dietary Context (Mediterranean Diet)
* **Healthy Fats:** Uses Extra Virgin Olive Oil.
* **Plant-Forward:** High volume of vegetables (zucchini, peppers, onions).
* **Aromatics:** Garlic, lemon, and herbs provide flavor without heavy sauces.
* **Portion Control:** Beef is treated as a secondary component to the vegetables.

**Serving Suggestion:** Serve with a side of quinoa or hummus for extra fiber.
