# Roasted Beet Salad

# Roasted Beet Salad with Goat Cheese & Walnuts

*Prep time: 10 mins | Cook time: 45 mins | Serves: 4*

---

### Ingredients
**For the Roasted Beets:**
* 3–4 large beets (red, golden, or a mix)
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 tsp salt
* ½ tsp black pepper
* 1 tsp fresh thyme (optional)

**For the Salad:**
* 4 cups arugula (or baby spinach)
* 4 oz goat cheese (soft, crumbled)
* ½ cup walnuts (roughly chopped)
* 2 tbsp balsamic glaze (store-bought or homemade)

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Roast the Beets
1. **Preheat:** Heat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. **Prep:** Scrub the beets clean (no need to peel them yet). Trim off the small green tops. Cut the beets into equal-sized chunks (about 1-inch pieces).
3. **Season:** In a large bowl, toss the beet chunks with olive oil, salt, pepper, and thyme.
4. **Roast:** Spread them out on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast for 35–45 minutes, tossing halfway through, until they are tender when pierced with a fork.
5. **Cool & Peel:** Let the beets cool for 10 minutes. The skins should slip off easily now—simply rinse them under cold water and rub them with your hands to remove the skins.

#### 2. Toast the Walnuts
1. While the beets are roasting, place the chopped walnuts in a small dry skillet over medium heat.
2. Toast for 3–5 minutes, stirring constantly, until they smell fragrant and look slightly golden. Remove from heat immediately so they don't burn.

#### 3. Assemble the Salad
1. In a large salad bowl, lay down the arugula.
2. Add the roasted beets and the toasted walnuts.
3. Top with crumbled goat cheese.
4. Drizzle the balsamic glaze over everything.
5. Toss gently to combine and serve.

---

### Cooking Tips:
* **The "Stain" Factor:** Beets will stain everything! To minimize this, you can roast them in aluminum foil, though roasting them "naked" results in a better roasted flavor.
* **Texture Balance:** Beets (Sweet/Earthy) + Goat Cheese (Tangy/Creamy) + Walnuts (Crunchy/Bitter) + Arugula (Peppery/Fresh).
* **Storage:** Store roasted beets in the fridge for up to 5 days.
