# Banana Bread Chocolate Chips

To add chocolate chips to your banana bread recipe, simply include ½ cup of semi-sweet chocolate chips as an optional mix-in. Fold them gently into the batter along with any other desired ingredients.

This modification is a common variation for those who enjoy a sweet and fudgy texture alongside the classic banana flavor.
